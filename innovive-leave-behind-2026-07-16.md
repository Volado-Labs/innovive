### 2. HubSpot Build-Out

You have HubSpot. We have access. Nothing is blocking us from starting.

What we would build:

- Contact and company records brought in, so the business owns the relationships rather than individual reps
- Website forms wired directly into HubSpot, so inquiries arrive as real records
- Automatic routing, so each inquiry reaches the right person without a manual handoff
- Follow-up reminders and task assignment, so nothing sits untouched
- Drip campaigns triggered when a new contact comes in
- Dashboards for pipeline and activity at a glance
- Training for your team

**Connecting NetSuite.** Your e-commerce runs on NetSuite, so your customer and order history already lives there. HubSpot is not worth much to your sales team until it can see that history, so connecting the two is what turns the CRM from an empty database into something people will actually open.

HubSpot publishes a native NetSuite connector, included on all HubSpot plans, that syncs contacts, companies, opportunities, invoices, orders, products, and cases. Whether it fits your setup depends on how your NetSuite account is configured, which we cannot tell from the outside. We would like 20 minutes with Rasan to find out. We will come back with a clear recommendation and cost after that, not before.

We will send a detailed roadmap after this call.

What we need from you: a yes to scope it, your reps' existing contact lists in whatever form they live in today, an introduction to Rasan, and one or two salespeople willing to join a working session. That last one decides whether the system gets used or ignored.

### 3. Content Research Briefs

We stop writing the posts. Your subject matter experts write them, and we do everything else:

- Keyword research and competitive ranking analysis
- Content pillar planning, so posts ladder up to a strategy instead of standing alone
- A research brief and outline for every post: the angle, the structure, the questions to answer, the sources, and the search terms it targets

Your SME writes from the brief. The words are genuinely theirs, which answers the concern that earlier posts read as AI-written. The brief means they are filling in expertise, not starting from a blank page.

Two briefs per week, delivered for review.

What we need from you: a named SME per pillar, and a realistic turnaround commitment. The briefs only create value if something gets published.
