## Honest Assessment Up Front

Innovive operates in a niche B2B market with very low search volumes across all product-specific terms. This is expected and normal for laboratory equipment sold to a small, specialized buyer pool (vivarium directors, animal facility managers, pharma/biotech researchers). The largest relevant term in the dataset is "iacuc" at 8,100/mo — but most terms Innovive should own run 10-170/mo.

**This does not mean SEO is unimportant.** It means:
1. Every visitor is a qualified buyer — traffic quality >> traffic quantity
2. The low KD across the entire dataset (avg KD 0-11 for core terms) means Innovive can rank for virtually everything it targets
3. The content strategy is about being the authoritative voice for vivarium operations, not driving mass traffic
4. Competing for higher-volume adjacent terms (IACUC, laboratory mouse, preclinical research) builds awareness with the research community even when they aren't actively shopping

## Strategic Note

Given the niche nature of this market, Innovive's content strategy has three goals that SEO supports differently:

3. **Establish thought leadership** — Vivarium operations, sustainability, animal welfare content positions Innovive as the knowledge authority in the space, which drives word-of-mouth in a small, referral-heavy market.
