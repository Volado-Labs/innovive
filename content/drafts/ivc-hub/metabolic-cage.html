How it works

Clean separation of urine and feces

A funnel design sends urine and feces into separate collection tubes, and disposable receptacles catch food crumbs and water drips so intake can be measured accurately.

1

Mouse on a wire-bar platform

The animal is housed singly on a wire-bar platform, with food and water in a wire-bar hopper.

2

Funnel and separator

A support cone with a single-use hydrophobic liner funnels output to a collection separator.

3

Two collection tubes

Urine and feces are collected in standard 15 ml conical tubes, removed without disturbing the animal.

4

Intake receptacles

Disposable crumb and drip receptacles collect spilled food and water for a more accurate intake measurement.

Why single-use matters for metabolic studies

  • No variability from collection surfaces that degrade with repeated washing
  • The hydrophobic liner helps ensure complete sample collection
  • Fewer pieces to clean and less storage space than washable metabolic cages
  • Recyclable components reduce contamination risk

FAQ

Metabolic cage questions

What does a metabolic cage measure?

It collects a single mouse's urine and feces separately and lets you measure food and water intake. In the Innovive cage, a funnel sends urine and feces to separate 15 ml conical tubes, and crumb and drip receptacles catch spilled food and water.

Is the Innovive metabolic cage disposable?

The cage bottom, lid, support cone, liner, separator, crumb catch and bottle are single-use and arrive irradiated in the M-MET-5 consumables kit. The wire bar, bottle holder, sipper tube and gasket are reusable (M-MET-KIT).

Can it go in an IVC rack?

Yes. The cage can stand alone on the M-MET-STAND or dock in the Innorack IVC Mouse.

What collection tubes does it use?

Standard 15 ml conical tubes, sold separately. They can be removed without disturbing the animal.

Is it for rats?

No. The cage is designed for a single mouse. Confirm no rat version
