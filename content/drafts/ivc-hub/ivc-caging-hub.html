FAQ

IVC caging questions

What is an IVC (individually ventilated cage)?

An IVC is a sealed cage that receives its own supply of filtered air and has its own exhaust, so each cage keeps a controlled environment. In the Innovive system, the Innorack delivers dual HEPA-filtered air to every cage position.

How many cages fit on an Innorack IVC rack?

Up to 192 on the Innorack IVC Mouse, up to 80 on the Innorack IVC Rat, up to 60 on the Innorack Tall IVC Rat and up to 24 on the Innorack Tall IVC Rat XL, depending on the row and column configuration you choose.

Are Innovive IVC cages reused?

No. Each cage is delivered irradiated and double-bagged and has never housed another animal. After use, cages are re-stacked with bedding intact and recycled through InnoCycle's closed-loop program instead of going to a cage wash.

Do Innocage® cages fit other manufacturers' racks?

The Innorack is designed for Innocage® caging. Innovive to confirm wording on retrofit and third-party racks

Can the racks run positive or negative pressure?

Yes. Every Innorack supports positive and negative pressure modes, with supply and exhaust blowers held at set points by the digital controller.

What water and bedding options are available?

Aquavive pre-filled bottles come in acidified, chlorinated or ultra-purified water. Cages can be ordered empty or pre-bedded with corn cob, ALPHA-dri or BioFresh.
