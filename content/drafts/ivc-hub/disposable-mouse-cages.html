Why disposable

Why labs switch to disposable mouse caging

Washable caging cycles through the cage wash: in, housed, out, cleaned, sterilized, dried and returned. At high study volumes that cycle becomes a bottleneck of labor, water, energy and space. Disposable caging removes it.

No cage wash

Cages arrive ready for immediate use or storage. There is no washing, sterilizing or drying between cycles, and no washroom capacity to plan around.

Animal-naive every cycle

Every cage is new, irradiated and double-bagged, so there is no risk from incomplete cleaning and no wear from repeated washing.

Recycled, not landfilled

Cage bottoms, lids, bottles and feeders are 100% PET. Recycle them locally or through Innovive's Closed-Loop Recycling Service, powered by InnoCycle.

How it works

One cage cycle, start to finish

1

Delivered research-ready

Irradiated and double-bagged, on your schedule. The inner bag is typically opened under the laminar flow hood where cage changes happen.

2

Assembled and racked

Add a lid, feeder and Aquavive® water bottle, then dock the cage in the Innorack IVC Mouse for individual HEPA-filtered airflow.

3

Changed every 14 days

Most users change Innocage® IVC mouse cages every 14 days, and some go longer with fewer than 5 mice per cage.

4

Recycled

Used cages are re-stacked with bedding intact and sent to recycling. No dumping, no washing.

FAQ

Disposable mouse cage questions

What is a disposable mouse cage?

A disposable mouse cage is used for one cage cycle and then recycled instead of washed. Innocage® cage bottoms arrive irradiated, double-bagged and ready to use, and they have never housed another rodent.

How many mice fit in an Innocage® M-BTM?

The M-BTM is sized for 5 mice, with 81 in² of floor space and 5 inches (12.7 cm) of headroom. That exceeds the floor space recommended for 5 mice in the US Guide for the Care and Use of Laboratory Animals and meets the European Type II Long specification.

How often are disposable mouse cages changed?

Most users change Innocage® IVC mouse cages every 14 days. Some extend beyond 14 days when there are fewer than 5 mice in the cage.

What are Innocage® cages made of?

High-viscosity PET plastic, BPA-free and free of glue or other contaminants. Cage bottoms contain up to 30% post-consumer recycled plastic and are fully irradiated after forming. The recycled content can slightly change the hue of a bottom, but not its clarity or quality.

Can disposable cages be washed and reused?

No. Washing is not an intended use for Innovive disposable cages, and reusing cage bottoms introduces the risk of contamination. Used cages can be autoclaved for decontamination; ask your Innovive representative for the protocol.

What bedding options are available pre-bedded?

Cage bottoms are available empty or pre-bedded with corn cob or ALPHA-dri®, and with corn cob enriched with Innorichment™. Pre-bedded mouse bottoms hold 450 cm³ of bedding. How It Works also lists BioFresh

Which racks do Innocage® mouse cages fit?

The M-BTM fits the Innorack IVC Mouse 3.5. The larger M-BTM-XL fits the standard Innorack IVC Rat, not the tall rat rack.

How long can mice stay in the cage if rack power is lost?

It depends on the lid and the number and size of animals. Innovive's power-failure planning guideline is 12 to 24 hours for the MVX1 lid, 12 to 24+ hours for MVX2, 6 hours for MVX3 and 48+ hours for MVX6.

How are used disposable mouse cages recycled?

Cage bottoms, lids, water bottles and feeders are 100% PET and are accepted wherever open-neck PET containers are recycled. Innovive's Closed-Loop Recycling Service, powered by InnoCycle, also picks up used cages from your facility on your schedule, so bedding does not have to be dumped.

What is the shelf life of disposable caging?

One year in the original packaging, stored dry and below 115°F (46°C).
