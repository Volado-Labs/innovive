 Gnotobiotics and the Innovive Research- Ready IVC System
 As microbiome research grows, maintaining gnotobiotic rodents in a germ-free setting is crucial. Traditionally, isolators have been the standard, but emerging studies support the use of IVC caging, especially disposable IVC caging, for effective germ-free maintenance
 Why Use a Research-Ready IVC System for Gnotobiotic Research?
 Research-Ready cages arrive irradiated, double-bagged, and are guaranteed to have never housed another rodent. This ensures aseptic conditions at the cage level from the very beginning, eliminating the possibility of germs being left behind from the cleaning process. Simply put, you start with a clean, germ free cage at every change
