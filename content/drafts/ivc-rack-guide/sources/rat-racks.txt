 Innorack IVC Rat System 
 All Rat Racks feature a highly-controlled clean environment at each cage level. 
 Portable, ergonomic, and easy to configure top-of-the-line ventilated rat housing system powered by patented transversal airflow and deliver dual HEPA-filtered air into each recyclable cage. Controlled airflow allows positive and negative modes of operation. It comes with a dynamic digital controller and provides a high-quality environment for your animals.
