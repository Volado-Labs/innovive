 Innorack Tall IVC Rat XL
 A highly controlled clean environment at each cage level
 It’s portable, ergonomic, and easy to configure top-of-the-line ventilated rat housing system designed to house Innovive disposable IVC cages. The rack is powered by patented transversal airflow and delivers dual HEPA-filtered air into each recyclable cage. Controlled airflow allows positive and negative modes of operation. It comes with a dynamic digital controller and provides a high-quality environment for your animals.
