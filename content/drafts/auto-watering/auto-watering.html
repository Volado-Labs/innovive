What it is

The Innovive system, fed by your water lines

Most Innovive customers give each cage its own Aquavive® water bottle. Some facilities already run automatic watering, where treated water is piped to every cage position on the rack. This setup lets those facilities use Innocage disposable cages without moving back to bottles.

It is three matched parts. A cage bottom with a grommet hole, a lid made for autowatering, and a rack with the water tubing built in. The cage itself stays single-use: it arrives irradiated and ready, and it is recycled after use instead of going through cage wash.

It suits facilities that are already plumbed for automatic watering, studies where a bottle change is a disturbance you want to avoid, and teams that want to cut bottle handling. CONFIRM positioning

Bottles or autowatering?

  • Aquavive bottles: no plumbing needed, pre-filled or empty, works on every Innorack.
  • Autowatering: no bottles to fill, change or recycle, uses your facility's own treated water.

Not sure which fits your facility? We can walk through both.

How it works

From facility water line to the cage

1

Connect the rack

The Innorack's water manifold connects to your facility's automatic watering supply. CONFIRM connection type

2

Prepare the cage

Insert the grommet into the M-BTM-A bottom and fit the MVX5 lid and feeder.

3

Dock the cage

Slide the cage into its rack position. The drinking valve passes through the grommet into the cage. CONFIRM valve position

4

Change and recycle

At cage change, pull the cage, remove the grommet and recycle the bottom and lid as usual. CONFIRM grommet is reused
