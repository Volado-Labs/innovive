# Innovive — Business Overview

**Website:** innovive.com
**Industry:** Lab Equipment / Vivarium Products
**HQ:** San Diego, CA

## What They Sell

1. **IVC Systems** — Individually Ventilated Cage racks and disposable recyclable caging for mice and rats
2. **Vivarium Services (Inno+)** — Animal husbandry staffing, inventory management, in vivo technical support (365 days/year)
3. **Gnotobiotic Solutions** — IVC system for germ-free mouse housing outside isolators

## Core Differentiator

**Cage-wash-free vivarium.** Disposable IVC caging arrives irradiated, pre-bedded, and double-bagged — research-ready from the box. Goes to recycling after use. Eliminates the cage wash room entirely: saves CapEx, space, labor, and ergonomic strain on vivarium staff.

## Target Buyers

- Vivarium directors
- Animal facility managers
- Institutional procurement officers
- Research institutions: pharmaceutical companies, biotechs, CROs, academic research

## Voice & Tone

Accessible and practical. Write like an experienced vivarium professional talking to a peer — not a white paper author. No unnecessary jargon. Lead with operational impact and real outcomes. Avoid dense technical language.

Key rules (from client feedback):
- Not too technical — rewrites were required when content was too dense
- No links to competitors or competing products
- All data/stats must be current and sourced from authoritative sources (NIH, USDA, AAALAC, peer-reviewed journals)
- B2B tone: credible, direct, solution-oriented

## Key Themes to Reinforce

- Reducing labor burden and ergonomic risk in the vivarium
- Biosecurity and contamination prevention
- Sustainability (recyclable/disposable caging vs. traditional wash cycles)
- Cost and space savings vs. traditional cage wash infrastructure
- Compliance with IACUC, AAALAC, and USDA standards
