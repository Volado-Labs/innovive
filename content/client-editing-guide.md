# Editing Your Innovive Website — A Quick Guide

A non-technical guide for updating text and content on innovive.com. You can change wording, swap images, add FAQs/events/case studies, and post news — all without code. **For layout, design, or structural changes, contact Volado Labs** (see bottom).

---

## 1. Logging in
1. Go to **innovive.com/wp-admin** (during the build: inno.myvoladolabs.com/wp-admin).
2. Enter your username and password.
3. You'll land on the **Dashboard**.

> If a page won't let you change the layout, that's intentional — your account is set up to edit *content* safely without breaking the design.

---

## 2. Two ways to edit — pick the simple one when you can

### A. Quick text edits (WordPress editor) — easiest
Good for fixing a typo or a sentence.
1. From the Dashboard, go to **Pages** and find the page (e.g. "FAQ").
2. Hover the page name → click **Edit**.
3. Make changes, then click **Update** (top right).

### B. Visual edits (Elementor) — see it as you change it
Best when you want to see the page while editing.
1. Open the page (as above) and click **Edit with Elementor** (top bar).
2. The page opens with a live preview. **Click any text** to edit it directly.
3. The left panel shows the text — type your changes; the preview updates live.
4. Click the green **Update** button (bottom-left) to save.

---

## 3. Common tasks

**Edit a heading or paragraph**
Click the text in the Elementor preview → edit in the left panel → **Update**.

**Edit / add an FAQ (FAQ page)**
1. Edit with Elementor → click the FAQ list (the expandable questions).
2. In the left panel you'll see each Q&A as an "item." Click one to edit its **question** (title) and **answer** (content).
3. To add one: click **+ ADD ITEM**, fill in the question and answer.
4. **Update**.

**Edit / add an Event (Events page)** or **Case Study (Case Studies page)**
Each event/case study is a **card**. Click its text to edit. To add a new one, the simplest path is to **duplicate an existing card**: right-click the card → **Duplicate**, then edit the copy. **Update** when done.

**Swap an image**
Click the image in Elementor → in the left panel click the current thumbnail → **Choose Image** → upload or pick from the Media Library → **Update**.

**Post a News article / Blog**
1. Dashboard → **Posts** → **Add New**.
2. Add a title and your text, set a **Featured Image**, choose a category, then **Publish**.
3. It appears automatically in News/Blog — no page editing needed.

---

## 4. Golden rules (so nothing breaks)
- ✅ **Do** edit text, swap images, add FAQ/event/case-study items, post news.
- ✅ **Always click Update/Publish** — changes aren't live until you do.
- ⚠️ **Preview before saving** big changes (Elementor: eye icon → Preview).
- ❌ **Don't drag, delete, or move** sections, columns, or boxes — that changes the layout.
- ❌ **Don't delete a widget** unless you're sure; if something looks wrong, click **Undo** (Elementor: the curved-arrow icon, or Ctrl/Cmd+Z) and **don't save**.
- ❌ **Don't edit the header, footer, or menus** here — send those to Volado.
