# Innovive — Content Feedback

Sourced from: Weekly SEO sync calls (Feb 5 + Feb 12, 2026), summarized by Collin Wood.

## Voice & Tone

**Too technical — required rewrites.**
The initial blogs were too technical for the audience. Vivarium directors and animal facility managers are experienced professionals, but the writing should be accessible and practical, not dense with jargon or academic language. Think: experienced peer explaining something at a conference, not a white paper.

- Write like a knowledgeable practitioner talking to another practitioner
- Avoid heavy use of acronyms without explanation
- Lead with practical outcomes and operational impact, not technical specs
- If something is complex, simplify it — don't lean into the complexity

## Competitor Links

**Do not link to competitors. Ever.**
No external links to competing products, brands, or vendors. If a relevant statistic or study needs a source, link to an industry body, government agency, or academic publication — not a competitor's website or product page.

## Data & Statistics

**All data must be current.**
When citing statistics, regulations, guidelines, or industry data:
- Always verify the most recent version is available (regulations change, study data gets updated)
- Prefer data from the last 1–2 years where possible
- Cite the original source (NIH, IACUC, USDA, AAALAC, peer-reviewed journals) — not aggregator sites
- If a stat can't be verified as current, don't use it — reframe the point without the number

## Summary Rules (apply to every Innovive blog)

1. Accessible and practical — not overly technical
2. No links to competitors or competitor products
3. Statistics and data must be current and sourced from authoritative sources (NIH, USDA, AAALAC, peer-reviewed journals)
