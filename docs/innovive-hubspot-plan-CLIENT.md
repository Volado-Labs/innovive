## 1. What we found

We audited your HubSpot portal directly. These figures are read from the live account on 6 August, not estimated.

| | |
|---|---|
| Contacts in HubSpot | 143 |
| Created by a form on your website | 141 |
| Contacts with an owner assigned | **0** |
| Contacts moved past the "Lead" stage | **0** |
| Follow-up activity ever recorded | **None** |
| Where they came from | 102 direct, **38 organic search**, 2 offline, 1 referral |

### The root cause

**One form on your website has been doing several jobs at once.** Sales enquiries, customer service requests, job applications, and general questions all submitted through the same Contact Us form, all landed in the same shared inbox, and all were written into HubSpot as a new "Lead".

On 4 August your site received six submissions. One was a job application, one was a genuine sales enquiry, and **four were existing customers reporting equipment problems**. All six were recorded identically.

### What that means for your numbers

The 143 figure is a count of form submissions, not leads. **26 of them are job applications.** Once those and customer service requests are separated out, genuine new-business enquiries are running closer to **5 to 10 per month**.

That is a smaller number, and it is the honest one. We would rather give you a figure that holds up in a board meeting than a larger one that does not.

**The good news:** the enquiries you are getting are exactly the right ones. Recent examples include the NIH, Battelle, the Pasteur Institute, Charles River, Zoetis, Vertex Pharmaceuticals, Sarepta Therapeutics, Penn State College of Medicine, the University of Michigan, and Children's Hospital of Philadelphia. The demand is real and well qualified. The gap is entirely in what happens after the form is submitted.

## 3. What we recommend

### 3.1 Tell leads and support apart inside HubSpot

`info@` stays as the single general enquiry route, with InnoPlus and careers on their own dedicated forms, and Sarah continuing to route what comes in. That keeps things simple for everyone already used to it.

The separation therefore happens inside HubSpot rather than on the website. Every incoming contact is assessed and marked as a genuine sales enquiry, a customer service request, or a job application, so your lead reporting stays accurate without anything changing for the sales team.

Job applications are already handled this way. They now go through their own form to `hr@innovive.com`, with a résumé upload and spam protection, and are no longer counted as sales leads.

If enquiry volume grows to the point where routing by hand becomes a burden, a dedicated customer service route is the natural next step, and it is easy to add later.

One small change on the Contact Us page in the meantime: make the employment note **bold and linked** to the job application page, and move it below "Send us a message", so people applying for jobs self-select before they submit.

### 3.2 Identify existing customers automatically

We import your NetSuite customer list into HubSpot as **non-marketing contacts**, which are unlimited and carry no cost. From then on, any enquiry from a known customer is instantly recognisable as an existing relationship rather than a new lead.

It is worth being clear about the limitation: a one-time export becomes a static list that ages. It is genuinely useful on day one and less useful three months later. It is worth doing as an interim step, but the durable answer is a connection between the two systems, covered in section 7.

### 3.3 Give every enquiry an owner

Today nothing is assigned to anyone. Each form will route to a named owner, so nothing sits unattended and it is always clear who is responsible.

### 3.4 Capture where enquiries come from

Three sources, working together.

**HubSpot already records this automatically.** Its tracking code captures the original source of every contact, which is how we know that 38 of your current contacts arrived through organic search. That is working today and needs no additional software.

**We add the "How did you hear about us?" question** you asked for, covering Google, LinkedIn, Instagram, X, trade shows and events, referrals, and distributors. It will be optional rather than required, so it does not add friction to the form.

**We tag every link you control**, using campaign tracking codes on your social posts, email links, sponsored newsletters, trade show QR codes, and all advertising from September onwards. HubSpot reads these automatically, so each enquiry arrives already labelled with the campaign that produced it.

Together these should bring down the 102 contacts currently recorded as "direct", which simply means we cannot yet see how they found you.

### 3.5 Record what happened

Each qualified enquiry gets an outcome recorded against it monthly, checked against NetSuite. This is what turns activity reporting into ROI reporting, and it requires no change to how the sales team works.

## 4. Events and trade shows

Between ten and fifteen shows a year, including European events, make this your largest source of contact with the market. At present those contacts are collected inconsistently and are not followed up in any structured way.

The missing piece is not the collection. It is what happens next.

The approach:

1. **Agree the categories first**, before loading anything, so every contact can be attributed correctly rather than tidied up later.
2. **Import consistently after each show**, whether it produced ten contacts or fifty.
3. **Follow through** with a defined sequence, so contacts are actually worked rather than stored.
4. **Attribute properly**, so each show can be judged on what it produced.

We suggest treating the **September event as the pilot**, with the two European shows following. The existing lists from earlier this year can be loaded as part of setting the process up.

## 5. Your subscription

You are on the Starter Customer Platform at roughly $45 per month across three seats, including 1,000 marketing contacts.

**We are not recommending an upgrade now.** The next tier is around $870 per month. It adds automation and lead scoring that solve a volume problem you do not currently have, and at 5 to 10 genuine enquiries a month the work is comfortably handled directly as part of your existing retainer.

**The number worth watching is marketing contacts.** You are at 141 of your 1,000, and only a small share of those are genuine marketing contacts today. Trade show lists are what will move that figure, since contacts you intend to email must be marketing contacts. When the allowance gets close, the answer is an additional block of contacts, which is a small incremental cost, not a tier change.

Some HubSpot features do sit above your tier, including workflow automation, lead scoring, and the built-in Google Search Console integration. None are necessary here, and the plan is designed so nothing depends on an upgrade.

**Worth revisiting toward the end of the year:** running the numbers across your full marketing tech stack, including Hootsuite, Supermetrics and Klaviyo. If consolidating those into HubSpot would genuinely reduce total spend rather than increase it, that changes the calculation. We are happy to carry out that review.

## 6. Email: keep Klaviyo

There is no native integration between Klaviyo and HubSpot, and we do not recommend building one at this stage.

Your Klaviyo list is around 5,000 customers, which exceeds the 1,000 marketing contact allowance in HubSpot on its own. Moving it would trigger a significant cost for no benefit, and would work against your requirement to keep customers and prospects separate.

**Recommendation: HubSpot owns prospects and new marketing. Klaviyo continues to own customer communications.** We will revisit only if it ever becomes cheaper to consolidate.

## 7. Answering "did marketing generate revenue?"

There are two versions of this, and we want to be straightforward about both.

**Deliverable for October, with no dependencies.** Each month we export that month's qualified enquiries, check them against NetSuite for closed business, and record the outcome in HubSpot. At current volumes this takes under an hour. It requires nothing from the sales team and no integration work.

**A better version, if it proves feasible.** A one-way, read-only connection from NetSuite to HubSpot that records closed business automatically, and lets us see whether an enquiry ever went anywhere. This depends on your IT team confirming it will not disturb the NetSuite work already in progress. We will provide a short written summary of exactly what would be involved, so that assessment can be made without us in the room.

We are planning around the first and treating the second as an improvement, so the October deliverable does not depend on it.

## 8. Reporting

**Monthly:** enquiries by type, genuine leads, sources, qualified handoffs, outcomes, and event contribution.

**Quarterly, for the board:** leads by channel, cost per lead once paid advertising begins, qualified handoffs, outcomes, and recommendations.

Its Supermetrics connections have expired, it has not been updated since the end of April, and its HubSpot section is drawing from the old Wix site rather than from HubSpot.

We will rebuild it as one dashboard pulling from Google Analytics, Search Console, HubSpot, Klaviyo for email performance, your social accounts, and event data. Before we do, we would like access to the existing Looker Studio dashboard so that every metric currently being tracked carries across and nothing is lost.
