# Innovive: NetSuite → HubSpot sync
Internal project reference | November 18, 2026

## What we’re trying to measure
- Identify whether a new HubSpot inquiry is from an existing Innovive customer at intake, before the individual necessarily exists in NetSuite.
- Follow a marketing touch (website, event, email, etc.) through to an actual purchase. Existing-customer purchases with a marketing touch count too, but should be reported separately from new-customer wins. A quote or a company-level association alone is not a win or proof of attribution.

## Current setup and meeting decisions
- Keep the integration one-way, NetSuite → HubSpot. HubSpot captures marketing contacts; sales qualifies and works them in NetSuite. NetSuite owns customer records, opportunities, and orders. Do not enable writes to NetSuite or broaden the contact import without separate agreement.
- Keep individual NetSuite customer/site records as separate HubSpot companies for now. The site linked by NetSuite is the operational relationship. A parent organization can have many sites on a shared domain; evidence of the organization is not necessarily evidence of the exact buying location.
- The contact sync is configured to match the same email on an existing HubSpot contact and bring over its NetSuite company relationship. That path does not need a company domain. If the organization exists but the person is missing from NetSuite, this contact sync alone cannot attach the person or identify customer status. The team discussed using a known customer’s domain to identify new people at that organization inside HubSpot; this is a separate matching path to validate, not confirmed live intake behavior. A blank/unmatched domain is unknown, not automatically a new prospect.
- On November 19, Collin narrowed the Marketing Leads view to Inquiry type = Sales inquiry AND NetSuite customer verification is either NetSuite confirmed noncustomer or Inactive in NetSuite. It showed 22 contacts at verification: 12 confirmed noncustomers and 10 inactive NetSuite records. Current customers, unknown/ambiguous customer status, jobs, support requests, vendor pitches, bots and unclassified inquiries are excluded. The view was previously the broader intake list. The 49 figure is a reported sync count, not a count of marketing leads or existing customers.
- HubSpot’s synced Deals represent NetSuite opportunities under the current setup. Opportunity = potential sale; estimate = quote; sales order = confirmed purchase signal the team wants for attribution; invoice = billing later. Some sales orders skip the quote. A contact and a deal sharing a company is useful for navigation, not evidence that the person generated the sale.
- Archived 3,554 unlinked non-Innovive companies, taking active companies from 7,232 to 3,678 at verification. Preserved 25 non-Innovive companies with associations and 142 with no subsidiary value, along with existing contacts and deals. The cleanup did not change future sync eligibility.

## Technical follow-up: what to verify for sales orders

- [HubSpot’s NetSuite connector](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/integrations/use-hubspots-integration-with-netsuite?t=1) maps Opportunities to Deals and Sales Orders to Orders as separate object syncs. The absence of an order in the Deals view does not by itself mean an order sync failed. The November 18 implementation below confirms an Orders sync was enabled. Next check whether the UCLA order is present in Orders. Do not alter the sync for this check.
- Ask Rasan for the NetSuite sales order internal ID, customer/site and contact, and any originating estimate or opportunity IDs. Oracle documents the transaction’s [Opportunity field](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1067079.html) and the sales order’s [Created From link](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N551392.html) to an originating estimate where applicable. Compare these sources with the synced HubSpot records; check which links actually carry over and whether the deal is directly associated with the correct marketing contact. A direct-to-order sale may have no opportunity or estimate to join.
- Confirm whether Victoria needs a simple confirmed-orders report or [HubSpot’s native revenue attribution](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/reports/understand-attribution-reporting?product=service). The native report requires Marketing Hub Enterprise, a closed-won deal directly associated with the contact, and populated deal amount, create date, and close date. A shared company alone is insufficient. Verify the portal tier and actual object associations before proposing any change. For an order-based report, use the NetSuite order ID as the deduplication key and only assign credit where the link to the marketing touch is supported.

## November 19 intake verification

- [HubSpot documents a form exception](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/object-settings/automatically-create-and-associate-companies-with-contacts): a submission containing company-object properties may associate a company even with global auto-association off or the domain excluded. If no company domain property is submitted, the contact email can match an existing HubSpot company domain in this form flow. With global automation enabled, HubSpot chooses one matching company when several share a domain; there is no configurable tie-break. Do not treat that choice as a NetSuite site match.
- The [June form audit](../content/hubspot-forms-plan-2026-06-16.md) lists a field named `company` and no explicit company-domain field. That is historical evidence; the field name alone does not establish that it belongs to the Company object rather than the Contact object. Inspect live field definitions and the actual form ID before drawing a conclusion about any current website path.
- Check an existing genuine web submission and a trade-show import separately, following their timestamps and creation/association history before creating any test records. For each, capture (1) HubSpot company association and timing, (2) organization-level customer status and its NetSuite evidence, and (3) exact site given by a synced NetSuite person/customer link. Use a unique known domain, a shared domain, and an unmatched control where feasible. An association answers none of the other questions by itself. Do not change global matching or submit synthetic production leads as part of the read-only review.
