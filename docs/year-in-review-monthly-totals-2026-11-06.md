## Read before using these numbers

1. **Google Analytics did not exist before 8 July 2026.** The current Google Analytics property was created on 8 July (the earlier one's tag was broken), so there are no session figures for June or 1 to 7 July, and they cannot be recovered. Google search clicks (Search Console) and form submissions (HubSpot) do cover June.
2. **July sessions are inflated.** July has 24 days of data but more sessions than any full month, almost all of the excess in Direct (1,064 in July against 357 to 452 after). That pattern usually means launch-week testing, internal staff traffic and bots, which were not filtered out yet. For a trend, use August to October.
3. **Form submissions for June and July used an older count** (every contact created in HubSpot). From August the count is website form submissions only. June and July may be slightly overstated against later months.
4. **Marketing-qualified leads for June are understated.** 23 of June's 29 submissions were never classified, so 2 is a floor, not the real figure. July has 5 unclassified, September 1, October 6.
5. **Social** is visits from social networks as Google Analytics records them. It is not likes or follower growth. Paid social is the LinkedIn ad campaign.
6. Organic search sessions (Google Analytics) and Google search clicks (Search Console) measure the same visitors two ways. They will not match, and the Search Console figure is the more complete one.

Not included: email (Klaviyo) and LinkedIn ad spend, which the dashboard reports separately.
